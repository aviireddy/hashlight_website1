02 About

Who runs
Hashlight.

One director, one point of view: a small studio that treats every account like it's the only one that matters.

Reddygari Aravind Reddy
Founder & Director, Hashlight
@avii.fashion

Reddygari Aravind Reddy started Hashlight after building his own audience from nothing; the account @avii.fashion now reaches millions of viewers a month, and every method Hashlight teaches and sells was tested there first.

Hashlight exists because most "social media management" is a posting calendar with no point of view. We think a reel is a small film: it needs a hook, a shape, a reason to exist. So we build a strategy before we ever pick up a camera, then we shoot, cut and publish it ourselves, with no handoffs, no guesswork.

Today the studio runs three arms under one roof: management for brands and creators who want consistent, strategic output; production, a bench of editors and shooters who turn ideas into finished films; and classes, where we teach the camera, scripting and editing skills we use every day.
